POOF — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: May 2026

POOF is a cannabis tracking and reduction app published by Frail Blue Dot LLC (Massachusetts, USA). This page describes how to delete your account and all associated data, in line with Google Play’s user data deletion requirement.

If you have an account (signed in with email, Google, or Apple)

  1. Open POOF on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile (Settings) tab.
  3. Scroll to the Data & Privacy section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm Delete Everything in the prompt. If asked to re-authenticate, enter your password.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Your Firebase Authentication account, all session logs, settings, and any other data stored under your user ID are removed.

If you used the app without an account (“Skip for now”)

  1. Open POOF on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile (Settings) tab.
  3. Scroll to the Data & Privacy section.
  4. Tap Clear All Local Data.
  5. Confirm Clear Everything in the prompt.

Anonymous users have no data stored on our servers — only on the device. Clearing local data wipes all session logs, settings, and preferences from this device. Your 21+ age verification is preserved so you don’t have to re-confirm on next launch.

If you can no longer access the app

If you’ve uninstalled POOF or can’t sign in, email us and we’ll process the deletion manually:

Email: support@pooftheapp.org

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include: The email address you used to sign in, and a sentence confirming you want all account data deleted.

Email-based deletion requests are processed within 30 days. We’ll reply when it’s complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (email, OAuth tokens)
  • All session and puff logs stored under users/{your-id}/puffs and users/{your-id}/sessions
  • All app settings and profile data stored under users/{your-id}/settings
  • All locally cached data on the device (for in-app deletion)

What we retain

Nothing. POOF does not keep backups, archives, or anonymized copies of your data after deletion. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

Questions

For questions about this process: support@pooftheapp.org
