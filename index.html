POOF

Track with data. Understand with science. Reach your goals.

POOF is a cannabis tracking and reduction app for adults aged 21 and older. Log sessions, watch your patterns, and work toward whatever goal makes sense for you — quit, reduce, T-break, or just understand.

POOF is published by Frail Blue Dot LLC in Massachusetts, USA.
